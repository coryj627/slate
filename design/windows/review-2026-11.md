## Alignment per family

**WinUI (matches the WPF Fluent theme):** Checkbox, Radio, MenuItem, MenuMenu, MenuSection (in a MenuMenu), NavNodeMedium, subtle icon-only Button 40 × 40, CaptionButton, TabView, ScrollBar, DatePicker, the type ramp, the 30% scrim, Dialog width.

**Web, close enough to WinUI (kept):** Button (WinUI pads 4/11/6/11, Web 12 each side), Input and Textarea, Dropdown, Switch (40 × 20 in both), Slider (WinUI thumb has a 12 px inner dot), ProgressBar (WinUI 3 px, Web 4 px), Tooltip, Accordion (Expander), MessageBar (InfoBar), SpinButton (NumberBox), TeachingPopover (TeachingTip), Dialog (ContentDialog), TreeItem, ListItem, Breadcrumb, HorizontalTab, Badge, Tag, Avatar, Persona, Card.

**Web only (no WinUI counterpart, or unused in Slate):** CompoundButton, SplitButton, Toolbar frame, Rating, Swatch and SwatchPicker, TagPicker, Carousel, ImagePreview, AvatarGroup, Skeleton, Toast, Drawer family, Popover, MaterialAcrylic (web-only). Keep them to Slate surfaces that need them; none change the WPF plan.

**Not adopted from WinUI, by decision:** two-layer shadows, the inner focus ring, 48 × 48 caption buttons, Mica.

## Consistent across the system (checked)

- Neutrals, text pairs, gold accent and selection pill are the same in cards, patterns and the Workspace kit, in light and dark.
- Menu rows: 40 px pitch, 34 px highlight inset 5 px, radius 3, check column kept on unchecked rows, in MenuItem, the Menu · States card, PanelToolbar's sort menu and the kit's app menu.
- Buttons and links centre their labels and share a 32 px height across primary, outline and subtle.
- Every card uses Slate copy and Segoe Fluent Icons; no kit placeholder text remains outside the Carousel and Toolbar reference frames.
