# Project decisions

- **Duplicate Figma families are intentionally not built.** The Fluent 2 kit repeats 56 component-set names (Avatar/Avatar ×5, .RadioBase ×4, Button ×3, …). Each distinct name is built once, so the "239 of 295 component families" design-system warning is expected. Don't materialize the duplicates.
- **Segoe Sans → Segoe UI.** Segoe Sans is Microsoft's web-only face and can't be obtained for the app. Its `@font-face` in `tokens/fonts.css` points at the installed Segoe UI. Don't ask for Segoe Sans uploads.
- **Segoe font files stay out of this repo.** The repo is public and the fonts' licence text limits them to the Microsoft product they came with. The tokens name the installed Segoe UI Variable families, and `tokens/fonts.css` reaches Segoe UI, Segoe Fluent Icons and Segoe MDL2 Assets with `local()`. Don't add them back to `fonts/`.
- **Accordion carries hand fixes.** `components/content/Accordion.jsx` and its compiled copy in `_ds_bundle.js` make `icon={false}` work in every variant and render `state="disabled"` collapsed, because the kit has no expanded disabled frame. Regenerating the component from Figma, or re-importing the design project without these changes, undoes them.
- **Eleven more components carry hand-fixed defaults.** Carousel, DataGridCellLarge, DataGridCellSmall, DataGridCellSmaller, Persona, AnimatedProgressBar, Folder, Placeholder6, RadioBase, MenuSection and NavNodeSmall had defaults that matched no frame. In each `.jsx` and in `_ds_bundle.js`, their defaults now name a real frame, some depending on another prop, and Placeholder6 also accepts `theme="regular"` for the frame Figma names `z`. Regenerating or re-importing undoes these too.
- **Negative gaps are negative margins.** Figma overlaps layers with negative spacing, which CSS ignores, so the 71 exported `gap: -N` values became a negative margin on the second child, in each `.jsx` and in `_ds_bundle.js`. Regenerating or re-importing brings the gaps back.
- **Link underlines are text underlines.** The export wrote each underline as a 3px element with a border on every side, which renders as a hollow box. Link, the DataGridCell family and TeachingPopoverContent now use `textDecoration` on the text, with the exported colour in `textDecorationColor`, in each `.jsx` and in `_ds_bundle.js`. The element's 1px of extra height is gone with it, so these frames are 1px shorter than Figma's. Regenerating or re-importing undoes this.
- **Skeleton's shimmer is a gradient.** The export lost it and left two solid rectangles. The inner layer now has the `stencil-1` to `stencil-2` gradient, in each `.jsx` and in `_ds_bundle.js`, and keeps each frame's offset. Regenerating or re-importing undoes this.
- **More hand fixes in generated components.** DataGridCell's link frames read `content` and SearchBox reads `placeholder` in every frame. MessageBar's message is regular weight with no underline, because Figma's mixed-style text was flattened into one string. StaticProgressBar, AnimatedProgressBar and Toast's bar lost their transparent borders, which left a fixed 2px or 4px bar no room for its fill. Spinner's label wrapper in its label-after and label-before frames fits its text. Props no component read were removed from Toast, NavBodyMedium, NavBodySmall, NavNodeSmall and TreeItem. Regenerating or re-importing undoes these too.
- **Collapsed frames have explicit heights.** The export gave some elements `alignSelf: "stretch"` inside a parent with no height, so they painted nothing. The Carousel step family and TeachingPopoverFooter's dots are `height: 8`, the value `Carousel` already used. SelectionSmall's boxes are 14px, ArrowProgress's images and PresenceBadge's glyph boxes are square, and LinkTags' divider box is 31px, with a 1px element in place of its SVG, because a zero-height `viewBox` disables rendering. TeachingPopoverFooter's dots also fit their own width. In each `.jsx` and in `_ds_bundle.js`; regenerating or re-importing undoes these. CheckmarkProgress has no image in any frame and no component uses it, so it stays blank.
- **Components keep their footprint across states.** Button, SplitButton and CompoundButton take the selected frame's 2px border out of its padding. ListItem's focus ring is an inset `outline`, so a scrolling list can't clip it. Switch and VerticalTab focus frames have a negative margin equal to what the ring adds, so the ring draws outside the same footprint. HorizontalTab's focus roots size to their content instead of a fixed width, then take the same kind of margin. The circular tabs' frames with a 1px border that rest lacks take it back the same way. These margins were measured to be constant across labels and hidden icons, so they don't depend on the default content. Input's `sm` rest, Slider's `sm` disabled, NavSubItem's hover and RadioBase's checked frames match their siblings' size. In each `.jsx` and in `_ds_bundle.js`; regenerating or re-importing undoes these. ImagesPreviewStep's larger selected thumbnail and StateTags' different labels keep their differences on purpose.
- **Avatar's and DEPRECATEDAvatar's image frames are a neutral circle.** They lost their picture on export and painted in the text colour. LinkTags gives its 28px avatar an explicit height.
- **Solid status fills keep white text.** `--neutral-foreground-on-brand` is near-black in dark mode, for text on the gold accent, so Badge's danger and success fills use the static white token instead. AnatomyBadge's fixed light fill has a fixed dark foreground, and StateTags' colours were corrected to reach 4.5:1. Regenerating or re-importing undoes these.
- **`Bell` is not ours to fix.** `components/internal/Bell.jsx` is a copy of Figma's own editor notification, with Figma's tokens, a fixed action row and low contrast. Nothing uses it, so don't build on it or spend effort correcting it.
- **Rating's mask layer is a clip.** Its frames exported the Figma mask as a painted rectangle. The filled star row now has a `clipPath` using the frame's padding, which is the unfilled width: display frames show 3.5 stars and interactive frames show 0, matching the kit's `0-5-stars` to `5-stars` token modes. Rating's `compact` default also depends on `type`, because interactive frames only exist with `compact=false`. Regenerating or re-importing undoes these too.
- **A combination with no frame renders the first frame.** The `?? __body0` fallback in the 204 generated components stays on purpose: these are reference frames, and a visible error would mean patching every component and the bundle. `readme.md` documents it. Don't treat the silent fallback as a bug to fix one component at a time.
- **Slate patterns are intentional additions.** `components/patterns/` (PaneSwitcher, PanelToolbar, NoteHeader, EmptyStateLauncher, CountListRow, SettingsLayout, SettingRow) were requested by the user and aren't in the Fluent kit. Ignore the checker's "named after nothing in the kit" warning for them, and keep them listed under Intentional additions in readme.md.
- **WinUI kit: only the listed pieces are built.** From the Windows UI kit, the system builds Checkbox, Radio, MenuItem, MenuMenu, NavNodeMedium and the subtle icon-only Button (rebuilt in place), plus CaptionButton, TabView, ScrollBar and DatePicker. The checker's "3,205 families" warning is expected. Don't import the rest (Windows shell, app icons, repeated sets, controls the Web components cover). Decisions are in winui-review.md.
- **The dark accent isn't logo gold.** The dark `--accent`, `--accent-text` and `--accent-indicator` are #EECF7E, with hover #F0D590 and pressed #EDCB75, in `tokens/colors.css` and `xaml/Slate.Dark.xaml`. Logo gold #E2AD24 measures |Lc| 59-62 as text on the dark surfaces, and the app's `ThemeTokenContrastTests` and the project standard need more than 75. The owner chose this on 2 November 2026. `--slate-gold` and the gold ramp stay as they are, for the logo. Re-importing the design project without this brings the failing colour back.
- **The reading view's selection is its own token.** A `RichTextBox` always draws selection on top of the text, so it can't use the solid `--selection-bg` pair. `--selection-overlay` and `Slate.ReadingSelectionColor` are gold at low opacity, chosen so primary text stays above Lc 75 under it. `xaml/README.md` has the measurements. Regenerating the theme without this key breaks the app's `ThemeManager` census.
