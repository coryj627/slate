The framework source explains why a directly created ListBoxItem container peer is an unreliable acquisition boundary. A ListBox exposes logical item peers and aggregates its visual container peers through EventsSource during its public child traversal. ProviderFromPeer checks EventsSource before StaticWrap validates connectivity. Thus a first wrap of an unprimed container can seek the wrapper that the tree does not expose, although that traversal initializes its aggregation for later calls. Canvas returns its custom card peers directly and does not have this ListBox wrapper/item split. [WPF 10.0.12 ProviderFromPeer and ValidateConnected](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dotnet/wpf/v10.0.12/src/Microsoft.DotNet.Wpf/src/PresentationCore/System/Windows/Automation/Peers/AutomationPeer.cs), [WPF ItemsControl child aggregation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dotnet/wpf/main/src/Microsoft.DotNet.Wpf/src/PresentationFramework/System/Windows/Automation/Peers/ItemsControlAutomationPeer.cs), [WPF public Selection provider implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dotnet/wpf/main/src/Microsoft.DotNet.Wpf/src/PresentationFramework/System/Windows/Automation/Peers/SelectorAutomationPeer.cs).
