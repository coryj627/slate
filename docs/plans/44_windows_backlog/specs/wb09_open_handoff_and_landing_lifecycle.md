Parallel with: WB-7, WB-8 (WB-8 touches `EditorInteractions.cs`, not these files). ## Goal

Three lifecycle holes a screen-reader user hears as a dead key, a stray focus change or a vault switching under a dialog. After this chunk: a second launch or jump-list open that arrives while a modal loop runs over the shell (or a palette command is sealed) is parked and runs once when the loop ends, the last request winning (#1296); an ordinary vault open — every asynchronous phase of it, from the session open to the scan, started from the Open button or from a recent vault — moves the keys exactly once, from the welcome view to the launch landing, never to the bare window, the button the reader pressed stays enabled under the keys, and a second press during the open restates the opening status instead of being a dead key or raising a second picker; a failed open leaves the keys on the welcome view (#1319, R-5 (h)); and a reading-mode tab's held F6 landing survives a rescan's in-place reload of that same tab under the very token the window's slot holds, seating and speaking once on the reloaded projection or being refused exactly once, while every other rebind still withdraws it (#1312, R-9/R-10).
